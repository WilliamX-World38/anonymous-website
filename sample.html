NIGERIAN ANTHEM

Arise all compatriots, Nigeria calls obey, to serve our fathers land, with love and trust and faith.

I pledge to Nigeria my country, to be faithful, loyal and honest, to serve Nigeria with all my strength, and defend her unity, to uphold her honor and glory so help me lord.

In the time of myth, and a time of magic, the destiny of a great kingdom lies on the shoulder of a young boy.His name is MERLIN.
